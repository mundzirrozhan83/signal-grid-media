Signal Grid Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Signal Grid is a tech and AI news page on Facebook and Threads. This policy explains what information our publishing tools handle and how. It covers the Signal Grid Facebook Page, the Signal Grid Threads account, and the Signal Grid app we use to publish to them.

What our app does

The Signal Grid app is used only by the Signal Grid team to publish our own news posts, pictures, videos and source-link comments to the Signal Grid Facebook Page and Threads account. It does not offer Facebook Login, and members of the public do not sign in to it or give it any information.

Information we collect

  • From visitors and followers: nothing through our app. We do not collect, store or sell personal information about people who view, like, comment on or share our posts.
  • Public comments: comments on our posts are stored by Facebook and Threads under their own policies. We may read them on those platforms to reply or moderate, but our app does not copy them into its own records.
  • Our own account data: the access tokens that let our app publish to our own Page and account. These are kept private and used only for publishing.
  • Published content: a log of the news stories we post (headline, source link, post time and post ID) so we do not repeat stories. It contains no information about visitors.

How we use information

Only to publish and manage Signal Grid's own posts. We do not use the app for advertising, profiling or tracking people, and we do not share data with third parties except the services that run our publishing: Meta (Facebook and Threads), and the hosting and automation tools that schedule our posts.

Cookies

This policy page sets no cookies. Facebook and Threads use their own cookies under their own policies.

Data deletion

Our app holds no personal data about members of the public, so there is nothing to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, or want a comment or mention removed from our posts, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Signal Grid is a general news page and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
